# Staff Knowledge Base Articles

These articles are published in the GLPI helpdesk as FAQ entries, visible to all staff through the Self-Service portal. They are written in plain English for non-technical users.

---

## 1. I'm locked out of my computer – what to do

If you see "The referenced account is currently locked out", your account has been locked after 5 incorrect password attempts. This protects your account from someone guessing your password.

**What to do:**

1. Wait 15 minutes – your account will unlock automatically.
2. If it's urgent, ask a colleague to raise an IT ticket for you, or call IT.
3. Once unlocked, type your password slowly and check Caps Lock is off.

If you get locked out again and again without typing a wrong password, tell IT. An old password saved on your phone or another device can cause this.

## 2. Finding your shared drives (P:, S:, W:, Q:, F:)

Your shared drives appear automatically in **File Explorer > This PC** when you log in. You only see the drives your department needs.

| Drive | Name | Who has it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P: | Public | Everyone |
| S: | Sales | Sales team and Management |
| W: | Workshop | Service team and Management |
| Q: | Parts | Parts and Service teams, and Management |
| F: | Finance | Finance team and Management |

**If a drive is missing:**

1. Sign out of your computer and sign back in (Start > your name > Sign out).
2. If it is still missing, raise an IT ticket and tell us which drive you need and why.

Please don't save work only on your desktop – save it to your department drive so it's backed up and your team can find it.

## 3. How to raise an IT support ticket

Go to **http://helpdesk01.ad.moonlab.internal** and log in with your normal computer username and password (Login source: **MOONLAB AD**).

Click **Create a ticket** and include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead (copy any error message, or attach a screenshot)
- When it started
- Whether anyone else is affected

**Choosing urgency:**

- **High:** you can't work, or a customer is waiting
- **Medium:** you can work, but it's slowing you down
- **Low:** a question or something that can wait

You'll get updates on your ticket, and we'll ask you to confirm when it's fixed.

## 4. Set up work email and Authenticator on your Android or iPhone

You will need: your work phone, your work email address and password.

**Step 1 – Install the apps**

- Android: open Google Play Store. iPhone: open the App Store.
- Install **Microsoft Outlook** and **Microsoft Authenticator**.

**Step 2 – Set up Authenticator (sign-in security)**

1. On a computer, go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo and sign in with your work email.
2. Click **Add sign-in method > Authenticator app** and follow the steps to scan the QR code with the Authenticator app on your phone.

**Step 3 – Add your email to Outlook**

1. Open Outlook on your phone and tap **Add account**.
2. Enter your work email and password.
3. Approve the sign-in request in Authenticator.

Your email, calendar and contacts will now sync.

If you lose your phone or it's stolen, tell IT straight away so we can protect your account.

## 5. Parts counter printer not printing

**Try these steps first:**

1. Check the printer is on, has paper, and shows no error lights.
2. On your PC, open **Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners > PARTS-Printer-01 > Open print queue**.
3. If a document is stuck at the top, right-click it > **Cancel**. Then try printing again.

**If it still won't print, raise an IT ticket. Include:**

- What you were printing (e.g. invoice, job card)
- Any error message
- Whether it's affecting other people at the counter

## 6. Got a suspicious email? Here's what to do

Scammers often pretend to be suppliers, banks, or management. Warning signs:

- Urgency: "Invoice overdue – pay today or account suspended"
- A sender address that doesn't match the company name
- Links or attachments you weren't expecting
- Requests to change bank details or buy gift cards

**What to do:**

1. Don't click any links or open attachments.
2. Don't reply.
3. Raise an IT ticket with the category **Security** and attach a screenshot of the email.
4. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, tell IT immediately – it's not a problem to report it, and acting quickly protects everyone.

If a supplier asks to change bank details, always call them on a number you already have – never one from the email.
